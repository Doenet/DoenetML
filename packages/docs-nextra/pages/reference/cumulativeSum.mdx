# `<cumulativeSum>{:dn}`

<ComponentDisplay name='cumulativeSum'/>

`<cumulativeSum>{:dn}` is a [Math Operator](../concepts/essentialConcepts#component-types)
component that renders the running total of its arguments: a list whose $n$th entry
is the sum of the first $n$ input values.

Where [`<sum>{:dn}`](sum) reduces a list to a single value, `<cumulativeSum>{:dn}`
produces a list the same length as its input.

<AttrPropDisplay name='cumulativeSum'/>

## Examples

### Example: `<cumulativeSum>{:dn}` of a list of numbers


```doenet-editor-horiz
<cumulativeSum>30 45 12 60</cumulativeSum>
```



Each entry is the total of everything up to and including it, so the last entry equals
the `<sum>{:dn}` of the whole list.



---

### Example: indexing into the result


```doenet-editor-horiz
<numberList name="pop">30 45 12 60</numberList>

<cumulativeSum name="cum">$pop</cumulativeSum>

<p>Running totals: $cum</p>
<p>After three subpopulations: $cum[3]</p>
```



The result behaves like any other list: individual entries are available with
`$cum[3]{:dn}`, and the whole thing can be passed on to another operator.



---

### Example: partial sums of a series


```doenet-editor-horiz
<p>Partial sums of <m>1/n^2</m>:</p>

<cumulativeSum name="partial" displayDigits="5">
  <number>1</number>
  <number>1/4</number>
  <number>1/9</number>
  <number>1/16</number>
  <number>1/25</number>
  <number>1/36</number>
</cumulativeSum>

<p>These approach <m>\pi^2/6 \approx 1.6449</m>.</p>
```



Rounding attributes such as `displayDigits` are passed through to every value in
the result.



---

### Example: symbolic partial sums


```doenet-editor-horiz
<mathList name="terms">x y z</mathList>

<p><cumulativeSum>$terms</cumulativeSum></p>
```



When the inputs are not all numbers, `<cumulativeSum>{:dn}` accumulates symbolically,
giving $x$, $x+y$, $x+y+z$.
