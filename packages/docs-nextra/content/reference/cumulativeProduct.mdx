# `<cumulativeProduct>{:dn}`

<ComponentDisplay name='cumulativeProduct'/>

`<cumulativeProduct>{:dn}` is a [Math Operator](../concepts/essentialConcepts#component-types)
component that renders the running product of its arguments: a list whose $n$th entry
is the product of the first $n$ input values.

It is to [`<product>{:dn}`](product) what [`<cumulativeSum>{:dn}`](cumulativeSum) is to
[`<sum>{:dn}`](sum).

<AttrPropDisplay name='cumulativeProduct'/>

## Examples

### Example: factorials


```doenet-editor-horiz
<cumulativeProduct><sequence from="1" to="6" /></cumulativeProduct>
```



The running product of $1, 2, \ldots, 6$ is the list of factorials
$1!, 2!, \ldots, 6!$.



---

### Example: compounding growth


```doenet-editor-horiz
<numberList name="growth">1.05 1.03 1.08 0.97</numberList>

<cumulativeProduct name="total" displayDigits="4">$growth</cumulativeProduct>

<p>Cumulative growth factors: $total</p>
<p>Overall: $total[4]</p>
```



Each entry gives the total growth factor through that period.



---

### Example: symbolic products


```doenet-editor-horiz
<mathList name="terms">a b c</mathList>

<p><cumulativeProduct>$terms</cumulativeProduct></p>
```



With non-numeric inputs the products are built symbolically.
