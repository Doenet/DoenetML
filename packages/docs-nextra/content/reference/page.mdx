# `<page>{:dn}`

<ComponentDisplay name='page'/>

`<page>{:dn}` marks the content of one printed page. On screen it is an
unformatted container, like a [`<div>{:dn}`](div). When the document is
exported to PreTeXt for printing, each `<page>{:dn}` starts on a new sheet of
paper, and the room left for writing answers (an expanded
[`<textInput>{:dn}`](textInput)) is stretched or shrunk, in proportion to the
heights written on the inputs, so that the page fills its sheet.

A `<page>{:dn}` does not start a count of its own: a
[`<section>{:dn}`](section) or [`<problem>{:dn}`](problem) inside it is
numbered along with the ones beside the `<page>{:dn}`. Inside a
[`<problems>{:dn}`](problems), the problems on each page are numbered as items
of the list, so a set of problems can be printed one per page by putting each
`<problem>{:dn}` in a `<page>{:dn}` of its own.

A document is printed in pages only when its `<page>{:dn}`s are written at its
top level, or inside a `<problems>{:dn}` (or `<exercises>{:dn}`) there. A
`<page>{:dn}` inside a [`<section>{:dn}`](section) prints as its content, with
no page break.

<AttrPropDisplay name='page'/>


## Examples

### Example: An exam with one problem per page


```doenet-editor-horiz
<page>
  <p>Name: <textInput /></p>
  <p>Show your work in the space provided.</p>
</page>

<problems>
  <page>
    <problem>
      <p>Solve <m>2x + 3 = 7</m>.</p>
      <textInput expanded height="3in"><label>Solution</label></textInput>
    </problem>
  </page>
  <page>
    <problem>
      <p>Factor <m>x^2 - 5x + 6</m>.</p>
      <textInput expanded height="2in"><label>Factored form</label></textInput>
    </problem>
  </page>
</problems>
```

The two problems are numbered 1 and 2, as they would be without the
`<page>{:dn}`s. Exported to PreTeXt and printed, the introduction, the first
problem and the second problem are each on a sheet of their own.
