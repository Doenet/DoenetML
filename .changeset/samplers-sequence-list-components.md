`<sequence>`, `<selectFromSequence>`, `<selectRandomNumbers>`, `<sampleRandomNumbers>`, `<selectPrimeNumbers>`, `<samplePrimeNumbers>` and `<sampleMultivariateRandomNumber>` now keep their values in one component, drawn as before, instead of making one `<number>`, `<math>` or `<text>` component per value, and a reference to all of them, such as `$s`, is one component as well. Documents built on random values and sequences, such as dot plots, load faster and use less memory, and dragging a point placed at a sampled value is faster. The values read as before wherever they are used: as children, through an index such as `$s[2]` or `$l[$n]`, in an attribute such as `displayDigits="$n"`, as their number changes, and in a `<repeat>`, a `<shuffle>` or an index into a `<group>` that holds a reference to them. A variant draws the same values as before. With `fixed="false"`, a value written to an entry, such as by dragging a point at `$ns[2]`, is kept for that entry and saved, until a sampler draws again or a `<sequence>` computes its values from another `from`, `step`, `type` or `exclude`; it is kept when a `<sequence>` only grows, and dropped from an entry it shortens past. `<selectPrimeNumbers>` and `<samplePrimeNumbers>` now take the display settings (`displayDigits`, `displayDecimals`, `displaySmallAsZero`, `padZeros`, `avoidScientificNotation`), as the other components do. Four uses differ. A `<numberList>` or `<mathList>` holding a reference to all of the values, such as `<numberList>$s</numberList>`, shows them with the display settings of the component, as a `<p>` does, where it used to show them with its own defaults. A selection that cannot be made shows its error after the select rather than in its place. A `<text extend="$s">` of a one-value `<selectFromSequence>` is a text holding the selection, which is not itself fixed; a write through it is still refused unless the selection has `fixed="false"`. An input bound past the last value of a `<sequence>`, such as `bindValueTo="$s[2]"` of a one-value sequence, shows nothing, as one bound past the end of a `<numberList>` does.
