A legend positioned at the right of a graph stays against the graph's right edge when the graph's limits change or the graph is resized. It used to run past the right edge or stop short of it. A boxed legend's box also stays sized to its labels in those cases, wherever the legend is positioned; a label used to spill out of the box or leave it too wide.
