A tag made with `extend`, `copy` or `copyWithState` from something it can't be is now an error when the document loads, where several such tags loaded silently:

- **A `<group>` made from a collection, or from one component that isn't a composite, is an error** (`doenet-e0065`). A collection is a `<repeat>`, `<repeatForSequence>`, a list (`<mathList>`, `<pointList>`, …), `<sequence>`, `<collect>`, `<sort>`, `<shuffle>`, or a `<select>` or other chooser that picks several: before, a group made from a repeat or a select of several showed nothing, with no message, and one made from a list, a `<sequence>`, a `<collect>`, a `<sort>` or a `<shuffle>` showed its items as the group's. One component that isn't a composite is a value, an input or an object (`<math>`, `<number>`, `<text>`, `<mathInput>`, `<answer>`, `<point>`, …) or a container (`<p>`, `<section>`, `<graph>`, `<problem>`, …): before, `<group extend="$m"/>` of a `<math>` showed the math, and one made from a `<choiceInput>` or an `<answer>` showed nothing, with an internal message about a state variable. The message names the forms that work: `<group>$r</group>` or `<group>$m</group>`, a group holding it, and `<repeat extend="$r"/>` or `<math extend="$m"/>`, the same component again, written with the attribute the author used. This holds also for a component named inside a section, a group, a case, an option or an iteration (`$sec.m`, `$outer[1].r`). A `<group>` made from a group (or a `<module>`), an iteration (`$r[1]`), a pick, a `<conditionalContent>` or a one-pick `<select>` is unchanged, as is one made from another composite, a property (`$P.x`) or an entry of a list (`$ml[1]`).
- **A `<repeat>`, `<select>`, `<collect>`, `<sort>` or other tag that makes its content itself, made from anything but the same tag, is `doenet-e0071`** however the source is written: `<repeat extend="$n"/>` of a `<number>` and `<repeat extend="$r[1]"/>` of an iteration were `doenet-e0065`, which named the source's type as though another type might work. It is now reported when the document loads also in a case that isn't true yet.
- **`<sampleRandomNumbers extend="$s"/>` of a `<selectRandomNumbers>` is `doenet-e0071`**, as the reverse already was, in place of a misleading "invalid children" warning. So is `<sequence extend="$s"/>` of a `<selectFromSequence>`.
- **An index into a `<conditionalContent>` is type-checked when the document loads**: `<point extend="$cc[1]"/>` of cases holding a text, and `<boolean extend="$cc[1]"/>` of cases holding a math, are `doenet-e0065`, as without the index. Before, they were `(0, 0)` with an "invalid children" warning, and a boolean with no message.
- **A scalar tag made from an iteration, a group, a case or an option holding a list whose entries are written out (`1 2 3`) is decided by those entries**, as `<math extend="$ml"/>` already was: more than one, counting what is beside the list, is `doenet-e0064`. `<math extend="$r[1]"/>` of `<repeat name="r" for="1">$ml</repeat>` showed a blank with no message, and `<text extend="$r[1]"/>` of one holding `<mathList>1 2</mathList>` the entries run together (`1, 2`). The message gives an index that names one entry, such as `$r[1][1]`. A list of one entry is that entry, so a `<number>` made from an iteration holding `<booleanList>true</booleanList>` is `doenet-e0065`, as from a `<boolean>`; it was `NaN`.
