A reference is now fixed, or has `fixLocation`, the same way whatever its form (`$P`, `$m.value`, `$P.x`, `$l[1]`, `extend` or `copy`): when it says so itself, or otherwise when its source is or where it sits is, including a `<group>`. A `fixed="false"` on its source or on where it sits no longer unfixes it; `fixed="false"` on the reference itself still does. An unlinked copy keeps whether its source was fixed when it was made, but not what fixed a sequence's entries or a repeat's value.
