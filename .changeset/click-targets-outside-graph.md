`triggerWhenObjectsClicked` now works for a `<text>`, `<number>`, `<label>`, or `<image>` outside a graph. The clicked object is shown with a dotted underline (a dotted outline for an image), and readers can also reach it with the Tab key and activate it with Enter or Space.
