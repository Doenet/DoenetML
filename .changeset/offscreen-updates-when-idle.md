Keep typing, clicking and dragging responsive on long documents by updating content that is out of view only when there is time to spare.

After any change, what is on screen, and just beyond its edges, updates right away. Content further up or down the page that depends on the change updates moments later, a little at a time, and gives way to the next keystroke or click. Scrolling to it brings it up to date first. On a document with many values computed from an input further down the page, a keystroke now takes less than half as long.

Answer checking and saved work are unaffected: they always use the current values, whatever is on screen.
